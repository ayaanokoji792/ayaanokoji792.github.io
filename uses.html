Things I use

The stuff I use every day, and what I actually think of it.

Hardware

  • Apple Watch SE 3 favourite

    The one I swear by. I wear it 24/7 and probably never will take it off. It's great for runs, workouts, sleep, calls and notifications, the workout metrics go into real detail, and it makes using my phone a hundred times easier.

  • iPhone 15

    Great camera and barely ever lags. Storage is tight, though, and the battery has started draining noticeably faster lately.

  • Ambrane AeroSync MagLite

    A magnetic power bank that works like a strap-on battery for my phone. It's a lifesaver on long days out with no charger around. 10/10 for usefulness, but I wish it charged faster.

  • JBL Live 770NC

    Great for travelling, bad for working out (I should've seen that coming). The noise cancelling is decent, but it should be better at this price.

  • Logitech MK345

    I bought this keyboard and mouse combo when I realised I needed a keyboard and my old mouse was already gone. It works great, it's comfortable to use, and I don't think I need anything better.

  • HP Envy x360

    My laptop, unfortunately. The battery life is abysmal. I wouldn't recommend it.

  • Casio G-Shock GA-2100

    My backup watch, though it's barely left the drawer since I started wearing the Apple Watch every day.

Software

Everyday

  • Brave

    Still my daily driver. I've been trying out Dia on my laptop and Arc on my phone, but Brave has kind of become a habit, and it's just easy to use.

  • Google Calendar favourite

    The best schedule tracker I've used. My Apple Calendar syncs to it and to my watch, and with the right watch face I know exactly what's coming up with one glance.

  • Gmail

    The mailbox.

  • Google Maps

    For whenever I need to get somewhere and have no idea how.

  • Apple Passwords

    My password manager and 2FA app. Works pretty well. Keep your passwords safe, guys.

Music, videos & reading

  • Apple Music favourite

    I mostly listen on my iPhone. I like the UI way more than Spotify's, and it sounds noticeably better (probably placebo, since it shouldn't matter over Bluetooth). The animated album art on the lock screen looks so good.

  • YouTube

    Half my day goes here.

  • Substack

    For when I want to actually read something.

  • Instagram

    Reels are addictive, and I'm guilty of being hooked.

Friends

  • BeReal favourite

    Probably the best social app I've ever used. It's a low-effort, easygoing way to stay in touch with friends, it stops passive lurking, and I love the give-and-take of it.

  • WhatsApp

    My main messaging app.

  • Discord

    Great for meeting people into the same stuff as me. Discord VC is also where my friends and I hang out when we're gaming.

Code & AI

  • VS Code

    My editor. I've also been experimenting with Antigravity and Claude Code.

  • Claude Opus & Gemini 4

    Unbeatable, for me at least.

Health

  • Apple Health

    Tracks my health, sleep and everything in between.

  • Nike Run Club

    For logging them runs.

Money & getting around

  • Google Pay

    I've tried a ton of other UPI apps. Most turned out to be gimmicks handing out promos, and nothing tops GPay for me.

  • Rapido

    My default for cabs and autos. Never regretted switching from Ola and Uber. Faster pickups, better prices.

  • Binance

    My crypto wallet.
